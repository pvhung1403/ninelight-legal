Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In short: Ninelight keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

To run your 3·6·9 practice, Ninelight saves the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

DataWhy
Your first name, affirmation and cycle settingsTo greet you and run your 21, 33 or 45-day cycle
Scripted lines, streaks and completed sessionsTo show your progress, calendar and streaks
Future-self letters, vision pins and logged signsTo show your journal, vision board and dashboard
Reminder times and theme choiceTo schedule your reminders and keep your chosen look
Your voice mantra recording (optional)To play your own affirmation back in a loop

None of this data is sent to us or to any third party. Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include it, according to your iOS settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap Record Mantra. The recording is saved on your device and is never uploaded. You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings.

Face ID

If you turn on the journal lock, iOS checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded; it never receives or stores biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push service or notification server is used.

Purchases

Ninelight Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we receive no payment details, only whether your purchase is active so the app can unlock Premium features. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. You can also overwrite your intention, delete letters and remove vision pins inside the app.

Children

Ninelight is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Because the app collects no data, changes will not affect data we hold about you — we hold none.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the App Support link on Ninelight's App Store page.
