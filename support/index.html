Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Need help? Open a support request (free GitHub account required). Please don't include private journal content. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

How does the 3·6·9 practice work?

Write your affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon and 9 times at night. A day counts toward your streak when all three sessions are sealed.

I don't get reminders

Open Settings → Notifications → Ninelight on your iPhone and allow notifications, then check the reminder times in the app's Settings tab.

Restore Premium on a new phone

Open any Premium feature to see the paywall, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ninelight. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I forgot the journal lock

The journal lock uses your Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. If Face ID fails, enter your iPhone passcode.

Where is my data?

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes its data.
